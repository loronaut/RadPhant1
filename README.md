# RadPhant1

**Rad**iomic **Phant**om version **1** was developed by researchers affiliated with BC Cancer and the University of British Columbia to evaluate radiomic feature consistency of cross-centre computed tomography images. 
Design files and fabrication details are available in SolidWorks and STEP format.

*Lorna Tu, Hervé HF Choi, Haley Clark, Bradford Gill, Scott Young, Samantha AM Lloyd; Evaluating consistency of radiomic features derived from CT images: a cross-centre phantom study, Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics (Accepted June 2025)*
